Last updated: October 2026

1. Data we collect

We collect the information needed to fulfil your order: name, email, phone, shipping address and payment details (processed securely by our payment partners — never stored on our servers).

2. How we use your data

Your data is used to process orders, provide support, personalise your shopping experience and — with your consent — send marketing emails. You can unsubscribe at any time.

3. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep your cart and session working, plus optional analytics cookies. Your cart and wishlist are stored locally in your browser.

4. Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting support@apnastore.com. We respond within 30 days.

5. Security

All traffic is encrypted with 256-bit TLS. We follow industry-standard practices to protect your data against unauthorised access.
